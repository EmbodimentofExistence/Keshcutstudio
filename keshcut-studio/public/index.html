Legal and privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Who we are

KeshCut Studio is a barbershop and barber academy in Bharatpur, Saptagandagi, Nepal. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you use this website or contact the studio through it.

What information is collected

  • Booking and enquiry details you choose to send. The booking form and the service links do not send anything to this website. They build a WhatsApp message from what you type (service, date, time, name) and open WhatsApp on your device. The studio receives your details, together with your WhatsApp name and phone number, only if you send that message.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the hosting service can see your IP address, device and browser type when the page loads. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a graphics library from cdnjs, so those services see the same technical data.
  • Analytics. No analytics tool is active at the moment. If one is added later it runs only after you agree (see the Cookie policy) and never receives your name, phone number, messages or booking details.

Why it is used

To answer your questions, to confirm, change or cancel appointments, to give information about services and academy courses, to keep the website working and secure, and, only with your consent, to understand how the website is used.

How it is handled

The studio uses your details only for these purposes. It shares them only with people who need them to serve you, with the service providers listed below, or where the law requires. The website does not keep a database of customers.

How long it is kept

The website keeps no booking details. Messages stay in the WhatsApp chat history on the studio’s phone and on yours until either of you deletes them. The studio keeps personal information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above. A saved cookie choice stays in your browser until you change it or clear your browser data.

Third parties

WhatsApp (Meta) delivers your messages and applies its own terms and privacy policy. The hosting provider serves the website. Google Fonts and cdnjs deliver fonts and code. Google Analytics 4 is used only if it is added later and you agree. Links to Instagram and Facebook lead to services with their own policies.

Your rights

Under Nepal’s Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) you may ask the studio what personal information it holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent you gave. Contact the studio using the details below. You may also use any complaint route the law provides.

Cookie Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What this covers

Cookies and similar browser storage that a website can use to remember information.

Essential technologies (always on)

The site works without tracking. It may keep your cookie choice in your browser’s local storage so you are not asked again. This is essential and does not identify you. The booking form does not store what you type.

Analytics cookies (off unless you agree)

No analytics is active today, so no cookie banner is shown. If the studio adds Google Analytics 4 later, a banner will appear first and analytics will stay off until you accept. It may then set cookies such as _ga to count visits and clicks on WhatsApp buttons. These events never include your name, phone number, messages or booking details.

Your choices

When the banner is shown you can choose Accept all, Reject optional or Customize. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer, or by clearing your browser data. Rejecting optional cookies does not affect how the site works.

Other requests

Loading fonts from Google Fonts and code from cdnjs is not cookie use, but it does send your IP address to those services (see the Privacy policy).

Booking and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Plain-language summary, not legal advice.

How booking works

  1. Choose a service in the list or fill in the booking form.
  2. WhatsApp opens with a message ready. Check it and add any missing details.
  3. Send the message.
  4. Wait for the studio’s reply.

Not confirmed until the studio replies

Pressing Book or Send on WhatsApp does not confirm an appointment. An appointment is confirmed only when the studio replies on WhatsApp to say so. Until then the time is a request and may be unavailable.

Cancelling and rescheduling

Please tell the studio on WhatsApp as early as you can if you need to cancel or change the time. The studio will reply with the options that are available.

Late arrivals

If you will be late, message the studio. What can be done depends on the schedule that day, and the studio will tell you on WhatsApp or in person.

Missed appointments

If you cannot come and have not told the studio, the studio decides how to handle it and will explain any rule to you on WhatsApp before you book.

Academy courses

Questions about barber training and academy courses are answered on WhatsApp, where course details are confirmed.
